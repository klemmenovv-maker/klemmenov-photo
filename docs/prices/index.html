Questions

What is the minimum booking?

Commercial and event shoots start at 2 hours. Personal shoots start at 1 hour.

Is the studio included?

No. Studio rental is not included in hourly shoots. Studio time is 315-500 AED per hour.

Which add-ons can I book?

Extra retouched photo, 10 AED per photo. Makeup artist, 700 AED. Makeup artist and hair, 1,000 AED per person. Video editing, 150 AED per video.

Can I add a second photographer or an assistant?

Yes. A second photographer is from 1,200 AED per hour and an assistant from 600 AED per hour.

What if I need to shoot outside Dubai?

Travel outside Dubai is 300-600 AED. The number of people, time and details are agreed in advance.
